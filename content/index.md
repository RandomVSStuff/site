# Welcome to Emberfall

It is the year of our Lord 910, a great invasion from unknown peoples have swept from the East, burning down everything from Kiev to Eire. A great migration has taken place to an new land far to the West, not many survived. His Holiness Pope Sergius III, and Patriarch Euthymius died, and left no successors, leaving the bishops to quarrel among themselves, with no clear hierarchy. It is not known which peoples made it to the new lands, which lords fled in cowardice, or what these new lands bring, but it is a new home nonetheless.

We invite you to join us on this server using the Conquest mod to build a world that comes from a historic time, but builds off into its own. Share a unique story, or partake in the stories others are building, and create a land that is truly living.
